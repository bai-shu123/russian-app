Large Russian-Chinese dictionary data
====================================

The offline index combines two WikDict/FreeDict data sets:

1. Direct Russian-Chinese dictionary
   Source: https://download.wikdict.com/dictionaries/stardict/
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

2. Chinese-Russian supplementary dictionary, reversed during the build
   Source: https://download.freedict.org/dictionaries/zho-rus/2025.11.23/
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode

3. OpenRussian morphology forms
   Source: https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary
   License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

The WikDict/FreeDict source packages state that they were generated by
WikDict, with base data from Wiktionary via DBnary. Direct Russian-Chinese
entries take priority; reversed entries expand coverage for offline browser
lookup. OpenRussian data is used only to resolve inflected forms to Russian
lemmas; its English translations are not displayed as Chinese meanings.
